Physics, not a prediction

Stars are massless tracers in two moving Plummer potentials. Their paths are integrated on the GPU with fixed-step kick–drift–kick leapfrog. The two halo centers follow mutual softened gravity on the CPU.

No stellar self-gravity, gas, dynamical friction or stellar collisions. Galaxies can pass, orbit and form tidal features; this model cannot predict a merger. Initial cold tracer disks are circular in each isolated halo, not a calibrated Milky Way or Andromeda model.

Code units: G = 1. Optional scale: length 10 kpc, mass 10¹¹ solar masses, time ≈47.15 Myr. This scaling is illustrative, not fitted observational data. One rendered point samples a stellar population, not one literal star.

Energy drift refers only to the two-center orbit. Individual tracer energy is not conserved in a time-dependent field. FPS is browser cadence, not GPU utilization.
